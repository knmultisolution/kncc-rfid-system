# KNCC RFID Fix - 2026-10-07

## RFID Cards page visibility fix

The RFID Cards page now loads cards in two ways:

1. Directly from `rfid_cards`.
2. Through the existing `students -> rfid_cards` relationship as a fallback.

The results are merged by RFID card ID, so a card that is already linked to a student and visible on the Students page will also appear on the RFID Cards page even when the direct RFID-card relationship query has a temporary PostgREST/RLS issue.

This is a web-page visibility fix only; it does not delete or alter existing student, RFID, or attendance records.
